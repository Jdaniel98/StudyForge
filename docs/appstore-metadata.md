## Description

StudyForge is a Pomodoro-based study timer built for serious learners. Track deep work hours per subject, build streaks, set weekly goals, and study alongside friends in private groups.

KEY FEATURES

- Pomodoro Timer — Customizable focus sessions with short and long breaks. Runs in the background with notifications when your session ends.

- Subject Management — Create subjects with custom colors and icons. Set weekly hour targets for each one and track your progress.

- Streaks & Heatmap — Build daily study streaks and visualize your consistency over time with a GitHub-style contribution heatmap.

- Weekly Goals — Set a weekly study target and watch your progress bar fill up. Adjust goals anytime from Settings.

- Study Groups — Create or join private study groups with invite codes. See who's studying and stay accountable together.

- Leaderboards — Compare weekly hours with group members or compete on the global leaderboard. Privacy-safe: members only see aggregated stats.

- Dashboard — Charts, streaks, today's summary, and weekly review all in one place.

- Home Screen Widgets — Glance at today's progress or your weekly goal right from your home screen.

- Siri Shortcuts — Ask Siri to start a session, check your streak, or see today's progress.

- CSV Export — Export all your study sessions for analysis in Excel or Google Sheets.

- Cross-Platform — Available on iPhone, iPad, and Mac with a shared codebase and synced data.

- Dark Mode First — Designed for late-night study sessions with a clean, distraction-free dark interface.

StudyForge is free and works fully offline. Create an optional account to sync data across devices and join study groups.
