Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 12, 2026

StudyForge for iOS and macOS

StudyForge ("the App") is developed by E Studio Software. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

1. Data Stored Locally

The App stores the following data on your device using Core Data:

  • Subjects you create (name, color, icon, category, weekly target)
  • Study session records (start time, duration, completion status)
  • Daily streak and weekly goal progress
  • App preferences (timer durations, sound and haptic settings)

This data remains on your device unless you explicitly enable cloud sync or join a study group.

2. Optional Account and Cloud Sync

If you create an account (via email and password), the App uses Supabase to store:

  • Your email address and display name
  • Study session summaries (subject category, duration, completion status)
  • Group memberships and aggregated weekly stats

Supabase hosts data on secure, encrypted servers. Your password is never stored in plaintext.

3. Study Groups and Leaderboards

If you join a study group, other group members can see:

  • Your display name
  • Your aggregated weekly study hours and session count
  • Your study streak length

Group members cannot see your specific subjects, session timestamps, session notes, or session-level durations. Aggregation is enforced server-side.

4. iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your local study data is synced via Apple's CloudKit to your personal iCloud account. This data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Analytics and Tracking

StudyForge does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking frameworks. No data is shared with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local data: Deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.
  • Account data: You can delete your account from the Profile screen. This permanently removes all your remote data, including sessions, group memberships, and profile information.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages. It does not collect personal information from children beyond what is described above. No age-gated content or social features target children specifically.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
