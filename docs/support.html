Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a study session?

Select a subject from the Timer tab and tap Start. The Pomodoro timer will begin a 25-minute focus session by default.

Can I change the timer duration?

Yes. Go to Settings and adjust the focus duration, short break, and long break lengths to your preference.

How do streaks work?

A streak counts consecutive days with at least one study session. If you miss a day, the streak resets. Check your current and longest streak on the Dashboard.

What can study group members see?

Group members only see your display name, weekly total hours, session count, and streak length. They cannot see your specific subjects, session times, or notes.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile and tap "Delete Account." This permanently removes all your remote data including sessions and group memberships.

Does the timer work in the background?

Yes. When you leave the app, the timer persists your end time and recalculates when you return. You'll also receive a notification when your session ends.
